## Format Performance (Instagram)

| Format | Avg Engagement | Best For |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Carousel** | **10.15%** | Saves, retention, B2B authority |
| Single image | 7% | Brand aesthetics |
| Reels | 6% | Discovery, non-followers |

- Carousels: **re-serve mechanic** — if scrolled without interaction, IG shows it again from slide 1
- Reels reach **2.25x more users**, 55% views from non-followers. Sweet spot: **60-90 sec**
- Stories: not for discovery — builds **affinity score** that boosts feed posts to followers

**TikTok:** 1-3 min videos actively boosted by algo in 2026.

## Carousel Best Practices

**Slides:** 7-10 optimal. Completion rate must stay >60%. Peak reach at slide 13 for long-form. Max: 20 slides.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (slide 1) — bold claim + curiosity gap. Exit rate highest here (23.8%)
2. **Problem** (2-3) — establish the bottleneck
3. **Solution/Steps** (4-6) — 1 concept per slide
4. **Proof** (7-8) — data, quotes, case studies
5. **Interactive** (9) — "Which resonates most?"
6. **CTA** (10) — explicit save/share/comment ask

**Pro tip:** CTA that sends users back to a previous slide = back-swipes count as extra engagement.

**Top-performing formats:** Step-by-step, "I was wrong", Before/After, Data dive, Myth vs Fact, Spot the difference.

**Design:** 1080x1350px (4:5 portrait). Inject 1-2 short video clips mid-carousel for dwell time boost.

## Hooks & Caption SEO

- **First 3 seconds** on IG / **first hour** on TikTok = distribution decision
- Captions > hashtags (semantic search in 2026)
- Front-load 3-5 long-tail keywords in first 2 sentences
- **3-5 hyper-niche hashtags** max — 30 hashtags = spam penalty
- Edit **Alt Text** manually with keywords (indexed by Google since July 2025)

---

## Maximize Saves vs Shares

- **Saves** → "referenceable" content (frameworks, checklists, data). Add visual "Save for later" sticker on last slide.
- **Shares (DM)** → Pass the "Send Test": would someone DM this to a specific person? "Send this to a developer who still writes boilerplate manually"
- DM automation: "Comment GUIDE → receive link via DM" (ManyChat)

---

## Growth from 0 Followers

**Instagram:** 70% Reels / 20% Carousels / 10% Stories. Use **Trial Reels** (test on non-followers first). Create content designed to be DMed.

**TikTok:** 30-day sprint (3-5/day). Target subcultures (#BookTok etc). Use Duets + Stitches + LIVEs.
